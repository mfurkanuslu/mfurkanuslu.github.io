# Privacy Policy for Soulo

**Last updated: June 2, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Soulo** application ("Soulo", "the app", "we", "us")
handles information. Soulo is a client for the third‑party **Soulseek** peer‑to‑peer (P2P)
file‑sharing network. Please read this policy together with the **Soulseek network's own terms and
privacy practices**, which apply whenever you use the network through Soulo.

> **Plain‑language summary:** Soulo does **not** collect, store, or transmit any of your data to
> the developer, and it contains **no advertising, analytics, or third‑party tracking**. Your
> Soulseek username and password are stored **only on your device**. To use the Soulseek network,
> the app necessarily sends your Soulseek username, a hash of your password, and your search
> queries to the Soulseek server, and — because the network is peer‑to‑peer — your IP address and
> username are visible to the Soulseek server and to other users you connect to. Soulo is
> download‑only: it never uploads or serves your files to the network.

## 2. Information Soulo handles

### 2.1 Information you provide
- **Soulseek account credentials** — the username and password you enter to sign in to the
  Soulseek network. These are stored **locally on your device only**, in the app's private storage,
  and are used solely to authenticate you to the Soulseek server. They are never sent to the
  developer or to any party other than the Soulseek network.
- **Search queries** — the text you type to search the network.

### 2.2 Information processed to use the Soulseek network (shared with third parties)
Because Soulo is a client for an external P2P network, using it necessarily involves the following
data being sent to the **Soulseek server** (operated by a third party) and/or to **other users
("peers")** on the network:
- Your **Soulseek username**.
- A **cryptographic hash of your username and password** (used by the server to authenticate you;
  your plain‑text password is not transmitted by the app to the server).
- Your **search queries**, which are distributed across the network so peers can answer them.
- Your **IP address and connection port**, which are inherently exposed to the Soulseek server and
  to peers you connect to in order to transfer files. This is a fundamental characteristic of all
  peer‑to‑peer networking and is required for downloads to work.

This information is handled by the Soulseek network and the peers you interact with, not by the
developer. The developer has no access to it.

### 2.3 Files you download
Files you choose to download are saved to your device's storage (e.g. the shared **Music** or
**Downloads** folder via Android's MediaStore, or app‑private storage). They remain on your device
and are not transmitted anywhere by Soulo.

### 2.4 Information the developer collects
**None.** The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell, or share any personal data. Soulo
includes **no analytics, advertising, crash‑reporting, or third‑party tracking SDKs**, and it does
not operate any backend server that receives your data.

## 3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the app's core functionality:
- to authenticate you to the Soulseek network;
- to send searches and receive results;
- to establish peer connections and download the files you select;
- to save downloaded files to your device;
- to show a notification while a connection or transfer is active.

Soulo does **not** use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to its
core file‑search‑and‑download function.

---

## 4. Information sharing and disclosure

- **The developer does not sell or share your personal data with anyone.**
- Data described in section 2.2 is exchanged with the **Soulseek server and peers** purely to
  operate the P2P network. This sharing is intrinsic to using Soulseek and cannot be avoided while
  using the network.
- We may disclose information only if required by law — however, since the developer holds no user
  data, there is in practice nothing for the developer to disclose.

## 5. App permissions

Soulo requests only the permissions needed for its function:

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| **Internet / Network state** | Connect to the Soulseek server and to peers, and detect connectivity. |
| **Foreground service (data sync)** | Keep the connection and active downloads running while the app is in the background. |
| **Post notifications** | Show the ongoing connection/download status notification. |
| **Wake lock** | Prevent the device from sleeping mid‑transfer so downloads can complete. |

Soulo does **not** request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or other
sensitive device data.

## 6. Data storage, retention, and deletion

- Your credentials and app settings are stored **only on your device**, in the app's private
  storage. They never leave your device except as the password hash sent to the Soulseek server at
  sign‑in (section 2.2).
- You can **delete your stored credentials at any time** by signing out within the app, or by
  clearing the app's data in Android Settings.
- **Uninstalling Soulo removes all credentials and app data** from your device. Files you have
  already downloaded to shared storage are not removed by uninstalling.
- The developer retains no data because the developer collects none.

---

## 7. Security

Your credentials are kept in the app's private storage area, which on modern Android is sandboxed
from other apps. As with any peer‑to‑peer software, your IP address is visible to the network while
you are connected; this is inherent to P2P and outside the developer's control. You are responsible
for keeping your device and Soulseek account secure.

## 8. Third‑party services

handle the information described in section 2.2.

providers**.
