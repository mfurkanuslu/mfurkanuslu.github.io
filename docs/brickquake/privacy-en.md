# Privacy Policy — Brickquake

**Last updated:** May 29, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game **Brickquake** (package name:
`com.mfu.brickquake`) (the "App") handles user data. The App is provided by the
developer **Muhammed Furkan Uslu** ("we", "us").

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## Data we collect

**We, as the developer, do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data
from you to our own servers.** The App does not require you to create an
account, sign in, or provide information such as your name, email, or phone
number.

### Data stored on your device
Your high score and game preferences are stored **locally on your device
only**. This data is not shared with us or any third party and is removed when
you uninstall the App.

### Data collected through advertising (third party)
The App is free and shows ads (interstitial and rewarded ads) via **Google
AdMob**. Google and its advertising partners may collect data to serve and
measure ads, including:

- The device's **advertising identifier** (Android Advertising ID)
- IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
- Device type, operating system version, and general device information
- Ad interactions (impressions, clicks)

This data is used for ad delivery, personalization, frequency capping,
measurement, and fraud prevention. This processing is governed by Google's
privacy policy:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses advertising data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Permissions

The App uses the following Android permissions solely to make advertising work:

- **Internet (`INTERNET`)** — to load ad content
- **Network state (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)** — to check connectivity
- **Advertising ID (`AD_ID`)** — to serve and measure ads

## Ad personalization and consent

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland
may be shown a consent screen (Google UMP / IAB TCF framework) for data
processing as required by applicable law. You can reset your advertising
identifier or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings:
**Settings → Google → Ads**.

## Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect data
from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child
has provided us with data, please contact us at the address below.

## Data retention and deletion

Because we, as the developer, retain no data associated with you, there is no
account data to delete. Data stored locally on your device can be removed by
uninstalling the App. For data collected by ad networks, please refer to the
Google policies above.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes take effect as

accordingly.
